## Expand and Contract

Stage schema changes by adding new structures first, dual-writing during the transition, and contracting legacy columns only after verifying consumers move off.

## Observability Is a Feature

Monitor query mix, error envelopes, and replication delay throughout the rollout to ensure the plan matches production reality.
