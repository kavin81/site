## Fault Boundaries First

Explicit timeouts, circuit breakers, and bulkheads stop localized failures from cascading across the fleet. Instrument these mechanisms and alert on their activation rate to detect early warning signs.

## Resilience Testing Cadence

Chaos drills and dependency rehearsal tests should be part of the release calendar so remediation playbooks feel routine instead of reactive.
